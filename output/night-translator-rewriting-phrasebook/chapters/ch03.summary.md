Before dawn Irena Vasko turned to page 212, Approved Terms of Address, and found Lenk at 212.4 and Vasko at 212.5. She told Brandt in her own words. He fetched a Form 9 and offered neither to recopy nor to settle it below the district. She completed it in ink; a carbon of Dora Lenk's identical form, eleven years old, lay in the cupboard, and the district's silence is all the post holds of her. At 05:20 Ana Petrik crossed outbound again; Brandt entered it in ink before the barrier lifted, and Vasko read page 212 as printed, her own name among the terms. Form 9 went into the district bag. She worked the last hour, keeping the count.
