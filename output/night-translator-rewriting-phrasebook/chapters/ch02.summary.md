Ana Petrik crossed outbound at 01:40 and back inbound at 03:10, same card, same purpose, answering in full. Both crossings entered in ink before the barrier lifted. At each crossing Irena Vasko found new phrases in the phrasebook's right-hand, traveller's column, addressed to the person holding the book, and read them as printed. From the cupboard ledgers she matched them to her own unlisted utterances, logged in Brandt's hand across eleven years and reissued as approved phrasing through the quarterly returns. In the oldest volume another hand: struck corrections, initials, ending mid-quarter. Brandt says that is how it has always worked. Vasko has told no one above and filed nothing. Open: whose hand, and where this ends for her.
