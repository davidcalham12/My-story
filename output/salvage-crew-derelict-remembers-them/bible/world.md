# World

Out past the last relay, a four-berth tug breaks dead ships for parts. The crew are contractors: they cut, tag, haul, and are paid by mass. The derelict they hook is an ordinary hull, decades cold, laid out like their own ship, its logs in their handwriting, their names on the bunk tags.

## Factions

- **The crew of the tug** — they want the salvage fee and a clean undock, and get it by cutting fast, logging everything, and refusing to discuss what the wreck says.
- **The registry office at the depot** — it wants every touched hull claimed and beaconed, and enforces this by withholding payment on unlogged wrecks and fining any crew that abandons one.

## Means

- **The cutting rig and the tag gun** — torch, spool line, and the numbered tags that turn dead ship into billable mass. Nothing is salvage until it is tagged.
- **The derelict's residual power** — a trickle: door actuators, cabin lights, printed text. Not enough for thrust, comms, or life support.
- **The written record** — the derelict's log books and bunk tags, handwritten, readable without power.

## Rules

- **R1.** The derelict knows only what the crew has already done or said aboard it or on the tug — names, habits, old arguments, private griefs. It may act on a habit it has seen (open the door a person always takes next, set a mug where they always set it) but never on anything not yet done or said, and it never predicts an event. Text in a crew member's hand reproduces only words that person actually said or wrote aboard either ship; it composes nothing new in anyone's voice.
- **R2.** It speaks only in writing and arrangement: printed text, lights, doors, objects placed as a person would place them. It has no voice and makes no sound beyond the ordinary working of the doors and lights it already controls.
- **R3.** Once a crew boards a wreck they must file a claim and leave a beacon, or forfeit the fee and pay the fine. Walking away quietly is not available.
- **R4.** Nothing aboard the derelict can be checked against outside record; the relay is a nine-day round trip and the book closes before any answer comes.
- **R5.** Anything cut from the derelict stays cut. No panel, page, or tag is restored once the torch has been through it.

## Texture

Cold metal, recycled air with a sweet edge. The horror is domestic: a mug set down where you set yours, a grievance in a hand you know. Nobody screams; everybody keeps working and is careful not to say it first.
