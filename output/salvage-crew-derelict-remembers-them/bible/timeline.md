# Timeline

The story's own span is about thirty-one hours, hook to undock. The depot relay is
a nine-day round trip (R4), so nothing filed in Chapter 3 can be answered before
the book closes.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Fourteen months before Chapter 1 | On an earlier wreck, Nell Varga orders the cut continued one tag short to make the mass quota. A spool line under tension takes Corey Dunn; Abe Whitlock is on the far end of that line. The death is filed as equipment failure and the file is accurate as far as it goes. |
| Fourteen months to nine days before Chapter 1 | Corey's bunk tag, mug and berth stay as they were aboard the Grebe. On night watches alone in the galley, Nell says the part she left out of the filing — out loud, more than once, to nobody. The Grebe is never empty of talk. |
| Chapter 1, hours 0–9 | The Grebe hooks an unpowered hull at the edge of the drift: same four-berth layout, decades cold, adrift longer than any of them have been alive. Bunk tags read Varga, Whitlock, Sen — and Dunn, in the right berths. Abe starts cutting anyway. Priya tags the first mass. A cabin light comes up over a berth nobody is standing in. |
| Chapter 2, hours 9–26 | The derelict's log books are handwritten in three hands the crew recognise as their own, plus a fourth, looping and undated by day. Doors open ahead of whoever is walking. A mug is set down where Nell sets hers. Priya says it out loud; Nell gives her a work-order instead of an answer. A printed page carries what Nell only ever said to the empty galley. |
| Chapter 3, hours 26–31 | Nell files the claim and Priya sets the beacon, because walking away costs the fee and a fine (R3). Nell tests the limit: she asks the hull in writing for something that was never done or said aboard it or on the Grebe, and gets a blank page. Abe cuts the confession page out with the torch; it stays cut (R5). |
| Nine days after undock | The relay's answer on the hull's registry would arrive here. The book has already ended; nobody aboard ever learns what the derelict was. |
