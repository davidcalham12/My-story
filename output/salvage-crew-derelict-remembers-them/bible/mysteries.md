# Mysteries

- **How can a hull that has drifted longer than any of them have been alive already know their names?**
  - True: the book does not explain the mechanism, and no chapter may supply one. What is canon is the boundary, and the chapters must hold it exactly: the derelict holds only what has been done or said aboard itself or aboard the Grebe — which is why the bunk tags were already right at boarding, since the crew had been talking on the tug the whole approach. It has nothing from their lives off the tug, nothing from before Nell's command, and nothing of the future. Asked for anything outside that, it produces blank paper, not a lie.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, the four bunk tags read in the right berths — three living names and Corey Dunn's — before anyone has spoken aboard the wreck.
  - Lands: Chapter 3, Nell writes out a question about something that was never said or done aboard either ship, gets a blank page back, and the crew undock with the boundary proved and the cause unexplained.

- **Whose name is on the fourth bunk tag, and what did Nell leave out of the filing?**
  - True: Corey Dunn, dead fourteen months. Nell ordered the cut continued one tag short to make the mass quota, and the spool line under tension took him; the official filing says equipment failure and is true but incomplete. She told no living person — she only said it aloud, alone, on the Grebe's night watches, which is precisely how the derelict has it. Abe was on the far end of that line and has suspected the shape of it without the words.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, the fourth tag is the one nobody touches and Abe is the only one who says the name aloud.
  - Lands: Chapter 2, a printed page in Nell's own hand sets out the quota, the short tag and the order, and Abe reads it standing next to her.

- **Do they cut it up and file the claim anyway?**
  - True: yes. Walking away forfeits the fee and draws a fine (R3), so they tag the mass, file the claim and leave the beacon. Abe puts the torch through the page carrying Nell's confession, and it stays cut (R5) — so the only evidence of the only thing they learned is destroyed by the same crew, and they leave the hull beaconed and claimed for whoever comes next.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, Abe begins cutting while the tags are still being read, because the fee is the reason they are out here.
  - Lands: Chapter 3, the claim is filed, the beacon set, the page burned out of the log, and the Grebe undocks with the mass and without an answer.
