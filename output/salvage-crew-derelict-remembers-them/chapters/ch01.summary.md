Chapter 1, hours 0–9, Priya's POV. The Grebe hooked an unpowered, unbeaconed hull, decades cold, laid out like their own ship. Before anyone spoke aboard it, Priya found bunk tags in the right berths: VARGA, WHITLOCK, SEN, and DUNN in the corner berth. Nobody touched the fourth tag; Abe alone said "Corey Dunn" aloud; Priya and Nell looked at the bulkhead. Abe rigged the torch and stated his cutting order: corridor plating, frames, berth deck skin, hold. Priya shot tag one into a bulkhead plate. A warm reading light came up over the empty corner berth. Nell gave eight hours forty left and said keep cutting. Nobody has said how the hull knows them. No log book opened yet.
