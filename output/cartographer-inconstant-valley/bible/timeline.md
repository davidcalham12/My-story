# Scarrowdale — Timeline

Day 1 is Thursday 11 May 1843. Rogation Monday is 22 May 1843, which is Day 12. All day numbers below are counted from Day 1 and are fixed.

| Day | What happens | Who knows |
|---|---|---|
| 1836 (before) | The enclosure plan is drawn by a surveyor who never walks the Rogation bound and never returns to the dale. It disagrees with the estate plan on the beck's course, with the turnpike plan on the lane, and with the county plan on where the parish stops. It is never confirmed; nobody tells him why. | Amos Dent, who was then a curate; the dale generally. Not the Commission. |
| Michaelmas 1842 (before) | Hollins Hall petitions the Tithe Commission to apportion Scarrowdale and offers to bear the cost of the survey. Silas Brock writes the petition and names Raven Hause as the head of the parish. The Commission appoints Edmund Hale and withholds his fee until seal. | Brock, the Commission, Hale. Nobody in the dale is told. |
| Day 1 — Thu 11 May | Hale comes in over Sile Ford, measures the ford stone as a check, and lodges at Nether Scarrow. Dent gives him the perambulation book from the church chest and declines to read a word of it aloud. Brock rides down from Hollins Hall the same evening to offer himself as a witness. | Everyone. |
| Day 2 — Fri 12 May | First chained line, Fowler leading. Fowler will not call the count; he puts the arrows in Hale's hand. The entry is taken at the station and signed by Brock and the Ingleford lad — the only two who will sign. | Hale, Fowler, Brock. Dent hears of it by evening. |
| Day 4 — Sun 14 May | Beacon poles set on Raven Hause and Sile Head; angles taken between them. Hale re-measures Day 2's line himself and it comes back identical to the link. He writes in the margin that the dale's reputation is slander. | Hale alone, in the book. Fowler saw him do it. |
| Day 6–7 — Tue 16 / Wed 17 May | Hale rides out over Sile Ford to Ingleford to post his interim return and have his chain compared to standard; returns the next afternoon. His first re-measurement after coming back differs from his own Day 2 figure by the width of a field. The column will not sum twice alike. | Hale. Fowler, who was on the chain and says nothing. |
| Day 8–9 — Thu 18 / Fri 19 May | Hale leaves the theodolite set up on Sile Head overnight rather than lose the sight; at dawn it faces the church tower. At that same dawn an entry is taken at the Raven Hause station in Hale's own hand, signed by Fowler's mark and by Dent, placing the head of the parish at the boundary oak — the estate's line, lawfully made, and Hale cannot remember making it. | Brock, Fowler, Dent. Hale only afterwards, reading his own book. |
| Day 12–13 — Mon 22 / Tue 23 May | Rogation Monday: the dale walks the bound. Hannah Rigg takes it wide, out round her three intakes, and puts her hand on the oak so Hale can carry the angle. A new entry correcting Day 9 is taken at the station and signed by Rigg and Dent, both of whom stood there. Next day Hale closes the survey on the church tower, the ford stone and the oak; the plan sums, Dent signs the bound, and it goes to Somerset House sealed — with Rigg's intakes numbered, tithed and hers. | The whole dale, and shortly the Commission. Brock has lost and is the first to congratulate Hale. |
