# Scarrowdale — World Bible

In 1843 the Tithe Commutation surveys reach the northern fells. Scarrowdale is the hundred's last unapportioned parish: a narrow dale a half-day's cart from Ingleford, with Nether Scarrow at its foot, the beck called the Sile running its length, Sile Ford at the mouth and Raven Hause at the head. Four plans exist — estate, enclosure, turnpike, county — and no two agree. The Commission calls that carelessness, and has sent a surveyor whose fee it will not pay until the plan is sealed.

## Factions

- **The Tithe Commission at Somerset House** — wants every field numbered and the plan sealed; withholds the surveyor's fee until then, and will apportion by estimate if none comes.
- **The Hollins Hall estate** — owns the grazing and pays for the survey; wants the bound at Raven Hause and the upper pastures entered as waste, which lowers its charge and enlarges its holding; gets it by hospitality, and by men who work slowly where it prefers nothing measured.
- **The dale's people** — want the valley left unnumbered, since what is written can be taken; keep it so by silence, by refusing to witness, and by walking the Rogation bound on a line no instrument has confirmed.

## Means

- **The Gunter's chain and its ten arrows** — worked by two chainmen; what the survey calls a fact.
- **The theodolite and the two beacon poles** — one on Raven Hause, one on Sile Head; the plan hangs on the angles between them.
- **The field book and the iron-gall ink** — entries made at the station and signed there; nothing in pencil has standing.
- **The parish perambulation book** — the record of the Rogationtide walk, kept in the church chest; the only account of the dale the dale accepts.

## Rules

- A figure repeats only for the surveyor of record — the hand that wrote it in the field book, not whoever held the chain under it — and only while that hand has not passed out of the dale at Sile Ford or Raven Hause. Once it has gone out and come back, that line never repeats again: every fresh attempt differs from the last. For any other surveyor of record it was never the same figure at all.
- Three things measure the same for everyone in every survey ever made, singly and in relation to one another: the church tower at Nether Scarrow, the ford stone at Sile Ford, and the boundary oak at Raven Hause. Angles taken directly among those three close identically, whatever lies between them. Everything else — walls, lanes, field corners, the course of the Sile — measures differently each time.
- An entry is official only if two witnesses who stood at that station sign it before the surveyor leaves the station. A correction to a signed entry is a new entry in every respect: two witnesses who stood at that station for the taking of the correction itself must sign it, and the original's witnesses count for nothing toward it, even if they are the same two people.
- No one born in the dale will say a distance or name a direction aloud, in their own words or read off a page. Nothing dale-born hands have written holds one to read: the perambulation book keeps the bound in durations and the names of dead men. Asked how far, they answer with how long the walk takes and who last walked it.
- Leaving an instrument out after dark is a thing no local does, not a thing the dale prevents. A theodolite left set up stands where it was put, and is found at dawn turned to face the church tower, no mark in the grass at its feet. The light going off Raven Hause is the dale's dusk and the light coming onto it its dawn, everywhere at once; no local will sign an entry taken at any station between the two.
- The Commission confirms a plan only if every numbered field lies inside the boundary as walked at Rogationtide and signed by the incumbent. A field outside the walked bound cannot be numbered.

## Texture

Wet bracken, low cloud on the fell shoulders. The dale is not menacing; it is courteous. Doors open, tea is poured, questions are answered with something adjacent to an answer. The wrongness arrives as paperwork: a column that will not sum twice alike, a signature nobody remembers giving. The calm is the horror.
