# Scarrowdale — Characters

Five people. Every one of them has a stake in whether the plan is sealed.

---

## Edmund Hale

- **Edmund Hale** — surveyor appointed by the Tithe Commission; methodical, proud of his arithmetic, quietly desperate

**Role.** Holds the commission for Scarrowdale: chain, theodolite, two beacon poles, one field book in iron-gall ink. Thirty-nine, from Lancaster, a road surveyor turned tithe man. Not born in the dale, and the dale knows it.

**Wants.** The plan sealed and the fee paid. The theodolite and the chain were bought on a note that falls due at Midsummer; unsealed, the plan is worth nothing and he is a bankrupt with instruments. Under that: he wants a column that sums twice alike, and he wants it more than he will admit.

**Will not.** Enter a figure he did not stand over. Sign for a witness who was not at the station. Pencil anything. He will lie to himself about what the dale is doing long past the point of evidence, but he will not falsify a book.

**Speaks.** In trade terms and flat declaratives — "Three chains, forty-one links, and I took it myself" — and asks the same question four ways when an answer slides off him.

**To the surveyor.** He is the surveyor. His private trap: after he rides out over Sile Ford on Day 6 and comes back, his own re-measurements stop repeating. He cannot leave the dale again without unmaking every line he has drawn.

---

## Silas Brock

- **Silas Brock** — land agent for the Hollins Hall estate; hospitable, tireless, entirely willing to sign

**Role.** Manages the estate's grazing and pays the survey's bills. Born in Lancaster, not in the dale, which makes him the one man always available to witness an entry — and available is his whole method. He does not obstruct. He arrives early with a lantern and a pen.

**Wants.** The bound drawn at Raven Hause and the upper pastures entered as waste of the manor. That lowers the estate's tithe charge and turns Hannah Rigg's intakes into Hollins Hall's rough grazing at Lady Day. He wants it signed, lawfully, with nothing to appeal against.

**Will not.** Threaten anybody where it can be repeated, or ask Hale outright for a false figure. He mentions a father's tenancy in passing and lets the man do his own arithmetic.

**Speaks.** Warm, procedural, always a half-step ahead — "You'll want a second hand on that, Mr. Hale, and I'm stood here already."

**To the surveyor.** Host, creditor of small favours, and the man whose signature is on more of Hale's field book than anyone's. Hale likes him, which is the difficulty.

---

## Amos Dent

- **Amos Dent** — incumbent of Nether Scarrow; keeper of the perambulation book, dale-born, slow to put his name to anything

**Role.** Vicar these eleven years; born at Sile Ford, took orders at Durham, came back. The parish perambulation book lives in the church chest under his key, and Rule Six means no plan is confirmed without his signature on the Rogation bound.

**Wants.** To sign a bound he has actually walked, and to sign it once. He half-believes the dale should stay unnumbered and wholly believes that an unsigned bound will let the Commission apportion by estimate, which would be worse for everyone.

**Will not.** Say a distance or name a direction aloud — he was born here, and eleven years of sermons have taught him to preach without ever pointing. He will not open the chest for Brock. He will not sign an entry taken after the light has gone off Raven Hause.

**Speaks.** In durations and dead men — "It's the length of the Creed at a walk, and my father walked it last."

**To the surveyor.** The one man who tells Hale the truth, in a form Hale cannot enter in a book. Gives him the perambulation book on Day 1 and will not read a line of it out.

---

## Nat Fowler

- **Nat Fowler** — leading chainman, dale-born; strong, literate, cornered

**Role.** Works Hale's chain with a hired lad from Ingleford, carries the ten arrows, born at the upper farms. His father holds his ground of Hollins Hall on a yearly tenancy. Nat can write his name, which in this dale is a liability: a man who can sign can be asked to.

**Wants.** His father's tenancy renewed at Martinmas, and to get through the survey without having been the one who gave the dale away.

**Will not.** Call a count aloud. He draws the arrows, sets them in Hale's palm, and lets the surveyor read the number off his own hand. He will not take the chain past the boundary oak at dusk.

**Speaks.** Little, and never in figures — "That's what's in your hand, sir. I'll not say it for you."

**To the surveyor.** Hale's best workman and his second witness of convenience. On Day 9 he puts his mark to an entry he knows will cost Hannah Rigg her intakes, and he does it because Brock mentioned Martinmas.

---

## Hannah Rigg

- **Hannah Rigg** — widow of the highest farm under Raven Hause; unhurried, unmoved, the only one who walks at Hale

**Role.** Holds three intakes of good grass above the beck that lie inside the Rogation bound as walked and on no plan whatever. Forty-six, widowed four years, farms with two sons. If the bound goes at Raven Hause she is a trespasser on her own hay.

**Wants.** Her intakes inside the walked bound — which means, appallingly, numbered and tithed. She has worked out before anyone else that the dale's silence, which has protected her family for a century, is now the thing that will take the ground off her.

**Will not.** Name a distance aloud, not even to save the fields; she is dale-born and it is not in her. Nor will she ask Dent to open the chest for her, because a favour asked is a favour owed to the wrong people.

**Speaks.** Short, courteous, faintly amused, answers adjacent — "How far? You'll be up by the time the milk's in, if you don't stop to look at it."

**To the surveyor.** Pours his tea, answers nothing, and on Day 12 leads him out to the oak and puts her hand on it so he can take the angle. Signs the correcting entry at the station with Dent, both of them having stood there.
