Days 12–13, Rogation Monday 22 and Tuesday 23 May. The parish walked the bound behind Amos Dent, who read the perambulation book aloud for two hours without one distance in it. At the top of the dale Hannah Rigg held her line where the path turned and took the walk wide round the Hollins intake, the Lang Riggs and the Little Waste, and sixty souls turned out after her and nobody said it was not the way. At the boundary oak she laid her hand flat on the trunk and held it there, and Hale took the angle off the back of her hand. At the Raven Hause station, with the light still on the fell, he made a fresh taking and wrote a new entry carrying the head of the parish to the oak by the bound as walked, the three intakes within it; Rigg and Dent, who had stood through the whole of it, signed before he touched the tripod. On the Tuesday he rebuilt the triangulation on the church tower, the ford stone and the boundary oak, numbered every field inside the walked bound, and added the columns twice to the same total for the first time. Dent signed the bound once. The plan went to Somerset House. Brock was first to his hand and paid to the penny. Hale measured the ford stone on his way out and rode up the Ingleford road without looking back.
