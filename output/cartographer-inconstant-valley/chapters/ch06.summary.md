Day 10, Saturday 20 May, at Hannah Rigg's farm under Raven Hause. She had the kettle on before Hale reached the yard and answered his question about the extent of her ground with how long her lads take to mow it — three days — and then with a walk from milking to milking. She would not give him a figure. She told him her eldest, up all night with a ewe, saw Silas Brock pass the low fold twice before the light on the Friday morning, the lantern lit both times and still burning on the second when the sky had greyed enough to see by. She said she did not know what it meant. She walked Hale out among her three intakes and named them: the Hollins intake, the Lang Riggs, the Little Waste. They are on none of the four plans. She said plainly that if the head of the parish is put at the oak and the tops entered waste of the manor, her hay becomes Hollins Hall's rough grazing and at Lady Day she is a trespasser on it. Hale said the only remedy is to have them numbered inside the bound as walked at Rogationtide, and that numbered means tithed and written down forever. She said number them. She will not ask Amos Dent for anything.
