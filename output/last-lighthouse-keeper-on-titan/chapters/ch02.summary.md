Days 2–3. Marit Anselm pulls the 2131 registration: her own voiceprint, her own custody signature on Wick-3, permanent and non-transferable — so the pod's beacon speaks in her voice, but the words are Halvard Ness's, composed as it sank on a re-anchoring run she ordered. Walking the custody stamps backwards she finds the handoff that never happened: Buoy 47's uplink horn tholin-fouled since 2138, rebroadcasting into an empty arc until Oyuna Brecht's anchor dragged it fourteen kilometres east, inside her mast's horizon. Uplink seconds bought from Delphine Sarr return the 2139 inquest's query filter: dead nodes excluded. Tomas Weir's cooperative is listening, salvage clock running. The fresh stamps are a radioisotope beacon, not a survivor. Ness died in 2138.
