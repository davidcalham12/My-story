Station 12, Titan, 2147 day 1. Marit Anselm, sole custodian of the last crewed light, works under Corvin Ladd's thirty-day-old decommissioning notice. A distress bundle arrives off the buoy chain, spoken by a voiceprint beacon in her own voice — her vowels, her throat catch — reporting a failing ballast seal in Nicholson Shoal. She has never said those words. She files it as a duplicate fault, then reads the custody chain: composed 2138, held all nine years by Buoy 47, a node the Authority lists as dead, with no stamp older than thirty days. She writes no theory down. She notes the *Sorrel Draught* under Oyuna Brecht crossing Nicholson Shoal seven days back against filed weather. A second bundle arrives, same voice, stamps four days old.
