# The Denominator

The *Vantage* has eight years of fuel left, and the people aboard cannot agree on where it should burn. Ninety-six years after the beam that launched them fell silent, a ship-born majority wants to keep its course for the world their ancestors chose from decades-old starlight; a smaller faction wants to divert toward a planet that looks wetter, more recently. The law is exact about who gets a say — and it says the 1,180 frozen founders count toward the total whether or not they ever wake to cast a vote. So before the ship can debate its destination, it must debate whether to open the vaults at all, knowing that revival is one-way and that not everyone survives it.

Juno Katsaros will run that vote and then disqualify herself from it, by the same rule she enforces on everyone else. Hanne Vollmer, who has kept the sleepers' record complete for sixteen years, has kept one page of it to herself. Oksana Brill counts water rations for a living and trusts nothing she cannot count herself. Around them: fifty days of testimony read aloud from the dead, a hall of hand-counted paper, and a warming clock on the first berths brought back — ticking down toward an answer the reader will get, and a cost the ship will not admit until it must.

Comparable to *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet*, *Aurora*, and *Seveneves*.
