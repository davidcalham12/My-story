Day 210, the counting hall. Juno Katsaros opened the Tally by reading her own disqualification aloud — Helm officers are struck from the roll while serving — and counted the paper chits in blocks of fifty, forty witnesses repeating each number back. No terminal was powered. She admitted a Continuance objection and a Trust objection in the same hour on identical grounds. Idris Falk quoted the cost in megawatt-hours and kilometres per second, and looked at her once too long. Result: 3,410 chits cast of 3,748; Wake 1,742, Keep Sealed 1,668, margin 74; Limited 1,441, Full 301. She sealed the hall herself; no recount. Falk offered her the chance to admit she wanted the signatories woken. She refused.
