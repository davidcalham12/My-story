# Timeline — the *Vantage*

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Flight year 0 (2211) | *Vantage* leaves Sol orbit under the launch beam. The 1,180 signatories are sealed into the sunward vaults. The Charter takes force with Article 9 written into it: a change of destination requires two-thirds of the chartered complement, and the frozen count in the denominator. |
| Flight year 96 | The beam goes dark without explanation at 0.0341c. Eighty-eight days later navigation issues an unordered trim command; the lockout follows, and shipboard computers become legally read-only in governance for good. |
| Flight year 209 | A power-bus fault lets berths 0901 to 0964 rewarm for nine days before trickle power is restored. The Charter Trust logs it as a thermal excursion and never tests it, because the only test is a revival that cannot be reversed. |
| Flight year 262, day 118 | Berth 0447 fails. The Trust attempts the only revival ever performed aboard; the signatory dies on warming day 31. Hanne Vollmer, then a vault technician, signs the death and the 9% figure stops being a statistic. |
| Flight year 271 | Vollmer, now First Custodian, finds sixty-four berths whose trickle signatures do not match the other 1,116. She seals the finding inside the Trust's enrollment record and tells no one outside the guild. |
| Flight year 283, day 12 | The Long Ear receives a Sol survey packet — at least 11.9 years old — showing surface water at Epsilon Eridani. The diversion argument begins the same week. |
| Flight year 285 | Continuance is constituted as a bloc under Oksana Brill. Idris Falk publishes the commit deadline: any diversion must spend 8.7 km/s of trim before flight year 292 day 1, leaving 32.5 km/s of the 41.2 to brake with, and pushing arrival from flight year 349 to 363. |
| Flight year 286, day 300 | The Helm sets the ballot for flight year 287 day 210 and names Juno Katsaros Returning Officer. Helm officers are struck from the roll. With minors and struck officers removed, the published roll is 3,748 chits — so the 3,527 an Article 9 diversion needs would be 94.1% of everyone who can actually vote. |
| Flight year 287, day 61 | Teo Marchetti's hydroponics audit fixes the ecology-neutral thaw at 190 berths: 4,110 awake plus 190 is exactly the 4,300 the closed loop sustains, and the biomass reserve is never touched. Brill calls it a betrayal from her own deck. |
| Flight year 287, days 154 to 203 | Noor Sandoval reads the testaments aloud in full for fifty days; 611 of the 1,180 turn out to contain a best-available-data clause. On day 203 Ansel Duong logs a Sol fragment that speaks of the beam consortium in the past tense. The counting hall is cleared the same evening. |
| Flight year 287, day 210 | The Tally. 3,410 chits cast of 3,748. Wake 1,742, Keep Sealed 1,668 — a margin of 74. Of the wake chits, Limited 1,441 and Full 301. The hall is sealed; there is no recount. |
| Flight year 287, days 214 to 254 | Vollmer names the first cohort and puts berths 0901 to 0964 at the head of it. Forty days of warming end with 0901 and 0902 opened on two people who died in flight year 209. |
