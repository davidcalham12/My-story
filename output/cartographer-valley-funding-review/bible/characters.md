# Characters

- **Nell Hartley** — the contracted cartographer sent out alone with a per-diem, six weeks and Notebook 12; methodical, stubbornly honest about what she cannot claim
  - Register: field-notebook plain. States the stone before the figure, dates everything, treats the valley's behaviour as a scheduling problem rather than a marvel. Her humour is dry and arrives as understatement inside an otherwise procedural sentence. She apologises to people and never to the ground.

- **Rosa Vane** — senior tenant of the upper holdings; holds the countersignature the Survey needs, and has withheld it for nine years; hospitable, unbudgeable
  - Register: courteous, unhurried, offers tea twice and information never. Answers the question next to the one asked. Speaks of the valley as one speaks of a difficult relative — fond, patient, with no intention of explaining it to the district office. When she finally explains anything, she does it in figures: levies, acres, years.

- **Sam Ord** — the previous contracted cartographer, who filed the eleventh sheet nine years ago and stayed; now a tenant who mends walls and knows all nine benchmark stones by their numbers; rueful, exact
  - Register: still speaks in survey vocabulary he has no professional use for — chainage, bearing, stone number — and corrects other people's measurements reflexively, then looks sorry about it. Deflects questions about his own sheet by describing the weather on the day he took it.

- **Arthur Penn** — desk officer at the Regional Survey, custodian of the grant file and the review date; present in the book chiefly as correspondence; punctilious, not unkind
  - Register: institutional letter-writing. Numbered points, deadlines restated, a closing courtesy that costs nothing. He cannot record uncertainty because the form provides no box for it, and he says so as though it settles the matter.

## Canonical spellings fixed here

- **Notebook 12** — Hartley's bound and numbered field notebook (Ord's, kept and never filed, is **Notebook 11**).
- **Form 9** — the countersignature page.
- **Stone One** through **Stone Nine** — the nine benchmark stones, always named by number word.
- **the Regional Survey** — capitalised; **the district office** — not.
