Four days before the deadline Hartley sorted Notebook 12 into stone-named readings (forty-one) and landmark bearings, and drew a sheet: Stone One to Stone Nine, the ridge, each reading named to its stone, acreage box empty. Sam Ord brought Notebook 11, admitted keeping the corrections out of his submission, said he stayed because the households pay off the signature he obtained, and asked that it be filed nine years late; Hartley recorded it and struck through *a tenant, name unknown*. Rosa Vane countersigned Form 9. Covering note to Arthur Penn: four numbered negatives. Last daylight reading, Stone Three, 417, held. Bundle handed to the carrier in light. After dark the ground let go of what the sheet declined to name.
