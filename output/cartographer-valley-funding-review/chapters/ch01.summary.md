Nell Hartley opened Notebook 12: date, weather, *unopened*. Arthur Penn's letter, five numbered points: one sheet, signed, no margin annotations, by the review date she copied in. Form 9 has boxes for surveyor, resident, acreage, none for uncertainty; she noted that in the notebook margin. Underlined from the brief: no resident countersignature in nine years. She occupied Stone Three a full day, nineteen readings, stone named first (Stone Three, 412; Stone Three, 406); three re-read at dusk came back identical, *holds exactly*. Rosa Vane met her with a lamp, offered tea twice (declined), talked weather, heard what Form 9 is for, said "No" without a reason, asked about a dry coat. Six weeks is thirty-one working days; one spent.
