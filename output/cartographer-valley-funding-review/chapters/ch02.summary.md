Two of thirty-one working days spent. Notebook 12 holds the Stone Three readings; at dawn the ridge notch read 417 against 412 and the wall corner 398 against 406, old figures struck through with a single rule, kept legible; the beck held. Nine strikethroughs so far; *Notebook 12 is filed with the map* written at the page top. Ridge notch from Stone Seven: 344. Sam Ord, mending a wall, corrected her chainage (two-eighty to two-ninety-one), named all nine stones, answered the eleventh sheet with the weather, and said its notebook is in the valley, in a drawer, numbered eleven. Rosa Vane, over tea, gave figures: 406 acres filed, one pound eleven the acre, nine years. Form 9 still unsigned. Hartley wrote the levy figures opposite her struck-through columns: the refusal is an invoice.
