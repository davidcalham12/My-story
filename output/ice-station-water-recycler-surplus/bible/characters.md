# Characters — Bittern Station

Canonical spelling. Every later stage is held against exactly these strings.

- **Nora Pike** — station chief and officer of the watch, the only person aboard who may key distress; signs nothing she has not watched accumulate for five days, keeps a second hand-annotated totalizer strip in her own pocket that does not match the logged one
- **Sam Okoye** — mine engineer, owns the cutting head, the 300 m heated slurry line and the single isolation valve; trusts valve positions and nothing that has a display, will spend airlock cycles he does not have to put a hand on hardware
- **Hannah Brede** — station medic and sole trained operator of the benchtop mass spectrometer; refuses to state a number without its error bar, will withhold a result she believes is within noise even when the watch is shouting for it
- **Theo Vance** — life support technician, runs Stack A at 60 % derate and counts the 180 kg of reserve LOX down by hand each morning; bagged and labelled the failed Stack B himself and has never opened the bag, argues for declaring and sitting in suits
- **Gil Marchetti** — recycler technician and Cold Harbor logbook custodian, the only crewman trained on the operator's entry schema; loyal to the form rather than to the reading, believes a discrepancy inside instrument tolerance is not yet a fact and has been rounding the loop inventory figure to keep it so

## Crew count — not a contradiction

The standing watch is six, as world.md states. Five are named above and carry the whole book. The sixth watch-stander is the relief hand **Salas**, surname only, spoken of and rostered but never given a viewpoint, a first name or a line of dialogue; Salas stands the 0200 watch, signs the strips he is handed and has no opinion about them. No other crew exist aboard Bittern.

## What each will and will not sign

- Pike will sign a five-day totalizer accumulation. She will not sign a tank level.
- Okoye will sign a valve position he has physically felt. He will not sign a flow figure taken off the summary screen.
- Brede will sign a D/H ratio with ± 10 ppm attached. She will not sign a conclusion drawn from one sample.
- Vance will sign the LOX burn-down and the derate. He will not sign anything that implies Stack A can be run harder.
- Marchetti will sign the daily schema entry, which is why his name is on eleven days of it.

## Fixed attributes

- Pike is on her second Bittern rotation; the other four are on their first.
- Brede's mass spectrometer is medical-bay property and its consumable sample vials are finite: twenty-two remain at the story's open.
- Vance and Marchetti share a compartment bulkhead and hear each other's pumps.
- Okoye advanced the cutting head into a new carbonate lens ninety-six days before the story opens and logged it as a face change, not as an assay change.
