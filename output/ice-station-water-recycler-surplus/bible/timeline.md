# Timeline — Bittern Station

Day 0 is the day the story opens: 180 kg of reserve LOX, a 2.0 kg/day shortfall, ninety days of air. Negative days are before the opening, positive days after.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Day −300 | The current watch takes over Bittern on a fourteen-month rotation. Pike signs as officer of the watch; Marchetti is handed the Cold Harbor logbook schema; the loop inventory is entered as 612 kg because that is what the previous watch entered, and no one has measured it since commissioning. |
| Day −96 | Okoye advances the cutting head off the worked face into a fresh hydrated sodium-carbonate lens in Vinalia Seam Four. He logs a face change. The assay is not rerun; the seam's bound water is not product and has never been counted as anything. |
| Day −40 | Electrolysis Stack B fails — membranes fouled, not aged. Vance bags it and puts it in the workshop. Oxygen production falls to Stack A alone at 60 % derate, 3.1 kg/day against 5.1 kg/day demand. The reserve dewars read 260 kg and the 2.0 kg/day draw begins. |
| Day −29 | Cold Harbor's automatic schema pull takes Bittern's stack-fault flag. Thirty-one minutes later the Consortium has it; within the day Kestrel Tug Line has it too, and loads a replacement electrolysis stack that nobody aboard Bittern has requested. |
| Day −11 | The potable loop returns more than it took: +2.3 kg on 61 kg throughput, about +3.8 %, against a ±0.5 % daily tolerance. Marchetti enters the day inside tolerance and begins rounding. It repeats the next day, and every day after. |
| Day −6 | Fifth consecutive surplus day. The accumulation is now loggable as a finding under station rule, and Pike's private strip and Marchetti's logged strip stop agreeing. Neither raises it to the watch. |
| **Day 0** | Story opens. Eleven straight surplus days, 25.3 kg unaccounted, 180 kg LOX, ninety days of air, twelve airlock cycles in the consumable budget, Kestrel sixty-one days out with a stack in the hold. Pike puts her strip on the table. |
| Day +61 | Kestrel arrives with the replacement stack. Reserve LOX stands at 58 kg — twenty-nine days of air — and under registry law a station that has declared distress before this moment has already lost the Occator claim to the ship that gets there first. |
| Day +90 | Reserve LOX exhausted at the unchanged 2.0 kg/day draw. Every full day Stack A spent offline before this date has moved it earlier by one. |
