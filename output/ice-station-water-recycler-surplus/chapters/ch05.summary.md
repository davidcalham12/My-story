Day +17. Theo Vance did the morning count by hand before touching the screen: 180.0 less thirty-four for seventeen days, less Okoye's 0.4, less the 0.78 of Brede's outage — 144.8 kg, 72.4 days, exhaustion Day +89. The screen agreed.

Then he pulled Stack A's cell-voltage record for the first time since Day −40 and found it was never a derate. 1.82 V at Day −40, climbing four millivolts a day, a straight line through fifty-seven points; the panel reads 2.05 this morning. Stack B's archived record — the file, not the bag, which he still has not opened — shows it quit taking current at 2.10 V. That is seventy days from Day −40: Day +30. After it, Stack A makes nothing and the draw becomes the full 5.1 kg/day. With 118.8 kg standing on Day +30, that is twenty-three days. Dead Day +53. Kestrel arrives Day +61. Eight days, on the wrong side.

Kestrel called at nineteen minutes of lag, pleasant, using the watch by name, confirming the replacement stack in the hold and offering to burn early the instant Bittern declares.

Vance did not take it to Pike. He tapped it through the bulkhead to Marchetti, who answered *tolerance*; Vance answered *slope*.
