Day 0. Nora Pike added the night's totalizer strip to ten she had been carrying: eleven straight days of outlet over inlet, +2.3 kg on 61 kg throughput, 25.3 kg unaccounted, against a ±0.5% daily tolerance. In the command module binder all eleven of Gil Marchetti's logged entries read loop inventory 612 kg and inside tolerance — a figure carried forward in the Cold Harbor schema, never measured by any instrument. Her strips and the station's had not agreed for six days and nobody had said so.

At the 0900 brief she laid both sets on the table and waited. Theo Vance named the difference in four seconds, read the burn-down off his own hand count — 180.0 kg LOX, 5.1 kg/day demand against Stack A's 3.1, a 2.0 kg/day shortfall, ninety days — and argued for declaring and sitting the rotation out in suits. Pike refused: distress is transmitted, irrevocable, and opens the Occator claim to the first hull alongside.

She opened a five-day finding and signed it: Okoye to the slurry line, Hannah Brede to the spectrometer with error bars, Marchetti to produce every schema entry since Day −11 unrounded. She left her own strip on the table.
