Day +6. Sam Okoye spent two of the twelve budgeted airlock cycles to reach the mine head, Salas tending. The two cycles vented 1.8 kg of atmosphere, 0.4 kg of it oxygen, leaving the reserve at 167.6 kg; the command module strip was re-cut that evening from ninety days to eighty-nine. Ten cycles remain in the budget.

Walking all 300 m with a glove on every jacket section, he found zone nineteen still running fourteen degrees high — the setpoint he raised on Day −14 to clear a slug and never lowered, logged as a slug and nothing more. At the isolation valve he confirmed full open by hand, stem out, and would sign that. Then, downstream of the inlet totalizer and on no drawing aboard, he found a fifteen-millimetre jacketed tie-in running into the recycler's vapour-compression stage: warm, trembling, carrying vapour rather than liquid.

He chipped two samples for Hannah Brede and labelled them by location and date only — fresh lens, and worked-face product off the billet rail — refusing to tell her instrument what it was measuring.

Inboard he gave the watch the sentence: the inlet totalizer is a liquid flow meter, the stub carries steam, and nothing aboard has ever counted steam as anything at all.
