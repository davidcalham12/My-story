Day +2, 176.0 kg LOX, eighty-eight days. Gil Marchetti printed eleven days of Cold Harbor schema and found his own signature under all eleven, each carrying field 14, LOOP INVENTORY (KG): 612. The field is mandatory; the schema will not take a blank and strips the provisional-value caret, and no comment field attaches to it. Chasing 612 backward, every entry read CF — carried forward — to the previous watch, and the watch before, down to commissioning, where the provenance column read DESIGN. The number was the loop's wet volume as drawn. No ultrasonic reading of the potable loop exists in station history and there is no fitting to take one.

He reconstructed his own rounding: +2.3 eleven times, 25.3 kg, every entry defensible alone because the form had nowhere to hold the sum. Then, in the transmission log, an unscheduled automatic pull at 0412 on Day −29 carrying the stack-fault flag he set himself on Day −40 — sent by no one, acknowledged thirty-one minutes later, and on Kestrel's manifest within the day.

He gave Pike the 612 and kept the pull to himself. The Day +2 entry opened with field 14 already reading 612, CF.
