Day 5, morning. [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] came over the last field to find both parents standing in the yard, his mother with her arms crossed and his father gone still. The sun was already too high. He said it before they could say anything: "I kept the rule." The sun touched the horizon while he was on the far side, so he stopped where he was and waited for morning and came home in the light, which is what the rule says.

His mother crossed the yard and gripped his arm. His father put a hand on his shoulder and it was shaking. Then he told them everything, out of order and all in one breath: the climb after breakfast with the whole day ahead, the place where he could no longer hear the village and did not know the way back, Bruno pressed against him and frightened, going on anyway; the valley nobody talks about, the stone house, Elena who is twelve, her grandmother Clara, the supper. Elena's fossil is a spiral, pale as a river stone, out of the riverbed. He set Captain Crunch beside it and looked at the two together, and he still has it. He left Elena one of his smooth stones — the striped one — to keep.

Then he saw his place at the table: the plate, another plate turned upside down on top to keep the warmth in, the cup gone cold. They had sat down to supper at the usual hour and kept his food warm all night.

His father looked at him and nodded once. His mother's hand went back into his hair and stayed there.

Still open: what Marcos knows. Marcos has not yet been told, and has not yet arrived.
