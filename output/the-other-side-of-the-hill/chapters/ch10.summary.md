Day 5, evening. [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] found his father in the kitchen and asked whether he could climb the hill again soon, with the same rule — home before dark. His father said yes, and asked whether he would follow it, and he said he would.

Rosa was at the gate, and had been waiting. Her words came out all at once, and she reminded him he had promised to tell her everything. So he told her: the path that got harder to see, Bruno pressed against his leg and whining, the thyme that rose in a smell when you trod on it, the quick grey birds calling from places you could not locate, how much steeper it was than it looks from below, the valley, Elena, her fossil, the night, the walk down at dawn. Rosa asked whether Elena was kind. Then she asked whether she could come next time, and he said yes.

He went in to Marcos. He could see it already — Elena standing in the doorway of the cardboard observatory, looking up at the painted stars — and then the fact arrived that it was not only his to show. He asked aloud: can I show Elena the observatory? Marcos tapped his stick twice and answered plainly, in one sentence: yes, she will want to see what he has made of the light.

They went out together. Inside the observatory they put Captain Crunch on the high shelf where the light falls and arranged the smooth stones beside it. Bruno slept in the dust through the open door. The stars came out.
