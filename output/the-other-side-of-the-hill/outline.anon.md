# The Other Side of the Hill — Outline

## Acts

1. **The Call** — Chapters 1–3 — [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] turns ten and asks about the hill; permission is given, but Marcos will not say what lies beyond.
2. **The Hill** — Chapters 4–6 — A first hesitant climb and then the real climb; fear on the path and Elena at the crest.
3. **Homecoming** — Chapters 7–8 — Arriving home changed; learning what Marcos was holding when he stayed silent.
4. **Understanding** — Chapters 9–10 — The fear made real and survived; the adventure opening to others.

## Promises to the reader

1. Bruno is a real character with his own wisdom and courage; he is not a mascot but a companion.
2. The dark is not a mystery to dissolve with magic; it is a real boundary that teaches respect and reveals what fear really is.
3. The greatest wonder is not magic but a real person living a real life on the other side of an ordinary hill.
4. Bravery is not the absence of fear but the choice to move forward despite it.
5. The small things we notice and collect—stones, fossils, stories—can connect us to another person across any distance.

### Chapter 5 — The Climb
- **Day and when**: Day 4, morning through late afternoon
- **Tension**: 8/10
- **Beats**:
  1. On Day 4 morning, they set out with the sun high and the whole day ahead; the air is cool and the stone path is clear beneath their feet.
  2. Past the shepherd's markers, where the path becomes unclear, Bruno moves close and whines; [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] feels his breath come short—he is not afraid of the dark, but of being lost and unable to bring Bruno home.
  3. They climb higher, and the fear sharpens, and then the ground levels and they step out onto the crest; below them lies a valley with a stone house, and a girl is standing in the yard watching the hill.
  4. As [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] takes the first step down toward her, the sun touches the horizon; Elena calls that they cannot safely descend now, and they must stay.
- **Plants / lands**: M4 (what he really fears—being lost, not darkness itself; he finds Elena and is found)
- **Rules in play**: R1 (cannot return in darkness; they stop instead), R2 (Bruno's fear is wise), R4 (darkness is coming), R7 (Elena's voice from across the distance)
- **Ends on**: Elena taking Bruno by the collar and leading them both toward the light in the house.

## Notes for canon

The timeline does not specify whether [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] and Elena part at the foot of the hill on Day 5 dawn, or whether Elena accompanies him further toward home. Chapter 6 assumes they part at the foot, as Elena lives on the other side of the hill. The "hours after dawn" arrival at home in the timeline allows for a solitary walk of some length from the foot to the village.
