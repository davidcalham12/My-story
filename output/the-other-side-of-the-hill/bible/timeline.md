# Timeline

| Day | When | What happens | Who knows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Morning | Leo turns ten. Marcos gives him a leather notebook for observations. | Family, Marcos |
| Day 1 | Evening | At supper, Leo asks his father directly about the hill. Father says he is old enough now, but must be home before dark. | Family |
| Day 2 | Morning | Leo gathers supplies: smooth stones from the jar, his fossil Captain Crunch, his new notebook. | Leo, Bruno |
| Day 2 | Afternoon | Rosa finds him at the village fountain and asks where he is going. He tells her he is going to climb the hill. | Rosa |
| Day 3 | Morning | Leo asks Marcos directly about the hill. Marcos says the hill itself will teach him what he needs to know. | Marcos |
| Day 3 | Afternoon | Leo and Bruno walk to the foot of the hill and explore the lower path. They climb a little way and turn back before attempting the full crossing. | Leo, Bruno |
| Day 4 | Morning | Leo and Bruno set out to climb the hill in earnest. The sun is high and they have the whole day ahead. | Leo, Bruno |
| Day 4 | Afternoon | They climb past the point where the path becomes unclear, into the stones and shade. Bruno becomes nervous; Leo feels real fear but continues anyway. | Leo, Bruno |
| Day 4 | Late Afternoon | Leo and Bruno reach the crest of the hill and see Elena's house and the land beyond for the first time. They meet Elena in her yard. | Leo, Elena, Bruno |
| Day 4 | Sunset | The sun touches the horizon. Leo realizes he cannot safely descend the hill in darkness. Elena invites him and Bruno to shelter. | Leo, Elena |
| Day 5 | Dawn | Leo and Elena descend the hill together by early light, Bruno moving between them. They reach the foot of the hill as the sun rises. | Elena, Leo |
| Day 5 | Morning | Leo arrives home dirty, tired, and hours after dawn. His family is waiting anxiously. | Family |
| Day 5 | Later | Leo tells his family about Elena, her grandmother, the house, and staying through the night. Marcos listens with a quiet smile that says he already knew. | Family, Marcos |
