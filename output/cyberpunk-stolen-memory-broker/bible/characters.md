# Characters — Baruna Reach

- **Nayla Wiryawan** — Slipway technician on the Estuary Seven pontoon clinic, protagonist; arithmetic-minded to the point of rudeness, unable to let a logged number stay wrong
- **Ilham Basri** — her old master and boat partner, the only other pair of hands licensed-in-all-but-paper at Estuary Seven; meticulous with a chair and with a cover story, owes drive stock he cannot buy
- **Oskar Maas** — dock buyer at the Ninth Floor Exchange who takes first-generation rips off the boats and signs their provenance; courteous, contractual, treats a person's ninety days as inventory ageing
- **Ratri Sundoro** — field assayer for Attestation Mutual, carries the calibration bench that decides what a memory is worth; pedantic about generation numbers, indifferent to whose life they came from
- **Juno Halim** — tower-side buyer at Ledge Tower who wears Lot 4417 and whose name is printed on its certificate; hungry for competence she was never taught, quietly frightened as the agency markers thin out
