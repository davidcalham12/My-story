# Timeline

Day 0 is the morning Chapter 1 opens. All port transfers are at 4 Mb/s (0.5 MB/s); one recorded hour is 360 MB, i.e. twelve minutes of unbroken cable. The Lattice averages six recorded hours of trace per day and overwrites at ninety days.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Fourteen years before | The Kanal Authority ties implant licensing to the power ration; clinics that cannot pay for a dry floor move onto boats. The Slipway trade forms in the eastern districts. |
| Nine years before | Ilham Basri fits nineteen-year-old Nayla Wiryawan with a Lattice in the Estuary Seven chair, then takes her on as apprentice. He keeps her connectome map on file; nobody thinks that is strange. |
| Two years before | Attestation Mutual publishes the generation tables. A certificate begins to be worth more than the file it describes. Ratri Sundoro moves from the bench room to field assay. |
| Day −163 | A courier boat is taken in the Ledge channel with a client's 194 GB archive aboard. Ilham Basri is liable for the drive stock. Oskar Maas offers to settle the debt in first-generation trace instead of fuel cells. |
| Day −117 | First "thermal-fault callback". Nayla is sedated in her own chair: 78 minutes of chair time, 6 minutes lost to four 90-second thermal hard-shuts, 72 minutes of cable — 2,160 MB, six recorded hours. Logged as no charge. |
| Days −108 and −99 | Second and third callbacks, identical logs to the minute. Two more six-hour blocks leave her port. |
| Day −88 | Fourth callback. The sedation runs shallow and Nayla surfaces for roughly six minutes: cable tension behind her ear, a rig fan, Ilham's hands. Her Lattice encodes that fragment — 36 MB, 72 seconds of cable to export. Total taken: 8,640 MB, twenty-four recorded hours. |
| Day −86 | Maas takes delivery at the dock on two courier drives, generation 1, and opens a provenance file under a buyer's name rather than a seller's. |
| Day −41 | Lot 4417 is transcoded into Juno Halim's connectome — 12% loss, now generation 2 — and fitted with forged agency markers rated to decay in 30 to 60 days. |
| Day −12 | Halim re-exports the lot as "first-hand". The Ninth Floor Exchange issues a certificate reading generation 1, detail 0.94, originator Halim, and lists it for sale. |
| Day 0 (Chapter 1) | Nayla reads the free scene index over the 9 kb/s induction link — text only, no images — and finds four days of her own life itemised under another woman's name. She does the cable arithmetic and finds it fits four callbacks in her own handwriting. |
