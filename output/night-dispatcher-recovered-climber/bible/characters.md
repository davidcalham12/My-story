# Characters

- **Ines Roth** — night dispatcher at the Kehlstein col post, 22:00–06:00, alone; literal-minded about procedure, slow to say aloud what she already believes
- **Karl Wenger** — climber logged RECOVERED on INSLOG line 0447, alive above the north couloir; methodical on the radio, stubborn about staying where his last fix was
- **Tomas Bauer** — Bergrettung Arnkogel duty controller, the only person who may set or correct a status line; defends his own logged decisions first, corrects them without excuses second
- **Lena Voss** — Wenger's climbing partner, the casualty actually recovered from the couloir at 15:55 Tuesday; careful planner, the one who swapped harnesses in the dark

## Canon facts

- The party is registered as two. PLB-7 tag **7-1142** is rented to Wenger; tag **7-1150** is rented to Voss. Both activated Monday 16:10.
- At the Sattelhaus, 05:52 Tuesday, they swapped harnesses. Wenger has worn **7-1150** since; Voss was recovered wearing **7-1142**. The swap is not in any log.
- Bauer set line 0447 — WENGER, KARL / RECOVERED — on field callsign **BERG 4** (15:55) and tag read 7-1142 (16:18), 23 minutes apart, inside the 30-minute window. The line is permanent; only a new line can correct it.
- Roth took the desk at 22:00 Tuesday and logs post checks on the quarter hour. She may not confirm a call she took herself, and Bauer is off the hill and asleep in the valley.
- Voss is carried on INSLOG as overdue, not recovered. Her tag, 7-1150, has kept bursting all night — and nobody reads that as strange, because they believe it is Voss who is missing.
- Roth has never met Wenger. She knows him as a name on a line, a voice on a tape, and a tag number.
