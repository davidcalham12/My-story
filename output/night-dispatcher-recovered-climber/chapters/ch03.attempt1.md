# Chapter 3 — A Correction Is a New Line

At 03:05 the callout tone opened on the valley channel, flat and two-second-cycled, twenty minutes past a post check she had not logged at 02:45. Roth put both hands on the desk edge and let it run.

It ran ninety seconds. Then the carrier broke and Bauer said her name, and after it: "Why is there no post check at 03:00?"

"Line 0463," Roth said. "Header stamp 23:12. Voice, male, naming himself Karl Wenger, unhurt, above the north couloir. Delivered to this desk 02:52. The Ostgrat hop was inside an Observatory window from 18:00; store-and-forward, twenty-minute retry, original stamp carried, no mark of delay." She stopped there. She had nothing after it she was permitted to say.

"Line 0447 is good," Bauer said. "BERG 4 confirmed one casualty at 15:55. Tag read 7-1142 entered 16:18. Twenty-three minutes. Both logged, both independent, inside the window."

The data channel went from hiss to chatter. Twenty-four bursts from 7-1150 landed in eleven seconds, stacked oldest first, the last stamped 02:42.

"Reading you the last fix," Roth said. "7-1150, 02:42, plus or minus 120 metres, under rock. Four hundred and ten metres above the top edge of the box searched for Voss. That search was suspended 20:40."

Silence on the channel, the length of a man finding a tape.

At 03:14 she heard her own night played back down the valley link — *this is Karl Wenger, I'm not hurt, I'm staying at my last fix* — and Bauer said, "Confirmation one. A voice, not a tag. I know what it is worth."

He worked. She heard pages, the register, a keyboard. At 03:29 he came back and his voice had changed register the way a log line does.

"Tag reconciliation. The transponder bursting all night is 7-1150. 7-1150 is rented to Voss. It is moving — four-minute cadence, 20:44 to 21:12 — four hours after I set a man recovered. The tag read off the casualty at 16:18 was 7-1142, rented to Wenger." A breath. "They swapped harnesses at the Sattelhaus. Nobody logged it. The live man is wearing her beacon and the casualty came down wearing his."

"Confirmation two," Roth said.

"Confirmation two, 03:29."

At 03:31 line 0471 came up on her screen in his hand, stamped by his clock and mirrored to hers: WENGER, KARL / LOCATED, ALIVE. VOSS, LENA / DECEASED, RECOVERED 15:55. Confirmations 03:14 and 03:29, seventeen minutes apart, inside the window, set by the duty controller and by no one else.

Above it, unchanged, line 0447 stayed where it had been since 16:20 Tuesday. It would stay there for as long as the log existed. A correction is a new line.

Team launched 03:40. She logged it: callsign, time, bearing, the 02:42 fix with its tolerance written out because a fix without one is invalid.

Then she logged the next line herself, in the plain wording the fault form wanted: post check due 03:00 not logged, dispatcher I. Roth, callout fired 03:05.

At 03:45, on the quarter hour, she logged the check.
