# Characters — The Titan Beacon Chain

Cast of six. Canonical spellings below; no later stage may vary them.

- **Noor Adeyemi** — keeper of Beacon Eleven, Shoal Point, on the northern shore of Kraken Mare, fourteen months into an eighteen-month Authority contract; compulsive log-keeper who trusts her instruments further than her own memory, physically incautious about heat budget when a checklist tells her to move
- **Emlen Pryce** — her predecessor at Beacon Eleven, dead sixteen months, posthumously convicted of abandonment on evidence that reached Enceladus long after his body cooled; meticulous to the point of annotating his own annotations, privately convinced the relay cache "kept things" and said so in logs nobody read until now
- **Iver Brekke** — Huygens Beacon Authority compliance auditor at Enceladus Station, the signature on the Pryce verdict and Noor's only official correspondent; procedurally merciless because the charter gives him no discretion, quietly unable to make the Pryce timings add up
- **Marta Okonkwo** — master of the Consortium skimmer barge *Tammuz*, twelve seconds of radar delay off Shoal Point, running below Convention weather minima on Consortium orders; risk-hungry and completely honest about it, will take a beacon's word over her own charter's
- **Sorrel Vane** — Drift salvage aerostat pilot of *Second Thought*, unregistered and uninsured, listening to distress traffic she is not licensed to hear; patient as weather, treats other people's archived voices as a navigational resource rather than as people
- **Jun Halloran** — Relay Trust duty engineer, stationed off Titan and never on it, reachable only as a cached packet a pass and a half old; incurious by temperament and contractually forbidden to describe the cache firmware, which makes him sound like a liar when he is merely muzzled
