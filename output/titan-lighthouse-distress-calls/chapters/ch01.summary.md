Contract Day 422, Beacon Eleven, Shoal Point. Noor Adeyemi wakes at 15:20 into the tail of a five-hour magnetopause blackout, finds the radar/heater alternation slipped, resets it by hand, and logs oxidiser at six percent beneath Emlen Pryce's sixteen-month-old handwriting. At 15:40 the first clear relay pass delivers an inbound distress call carrying Beacon Eleven's own station hash, spoken in her voice with commissioning-booth cadence, giving a position report she never made. The packet is stamped 1:18 ahead of station time; the station auto-acknowledged and forwarded it before she reached the console. She logs it verbatim, adds no interpretation, and may not power down a lit beacon. She does not yet know the mechanism.
