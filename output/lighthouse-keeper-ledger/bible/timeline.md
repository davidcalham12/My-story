# Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Ninety years before the story | The Corbie Light is first lit. Page 1 of the Register of Passage is opened and has been written in every night since. |
| 3 March, eleven years ago, 3 a.m. | Walter Byrd enters the *Marigold* on page 91 — *three of the clock, sou'-sou'-west, Marigold, ketch*. At the flood, four hours later, he is taken off the causeway and drowned. Ada Rowe stands the rest of that watch and writes nothing. |
| 3 March, eleven years ago, 10 p.m. | The *Marigold* sails, nineteen hours after the line that records her passing, and is lost with four hands on the Sallowmere shoal. The Commissioners hold no return for her; Walter's line is her first entry. |
| Nine years ago, first spring tide of autumn | Ada Rowe begins copying names off the petitions left on the seventh step and entering them as sightings. The *Marigold* is entered a second time. Nell Harker starts leaving a petition at every low water. |
| Two years ago | Thomas Creed takes the audit and first challenges the unmatched names. He warns Ada that a third *Marigold* sends the name to the Drowned Page for good. |
| 14 March, present — low water, afternoon (Chapter 1) | Nell's petition is on the seventh step: the *Marigold*, a third time. Creed comes up the causeway to give notice of the spring-tide audit in two days. |
| 15 March, present — night (Chapter 2) | Ada opens the Register to page 91 and finds Walter's hand where she has never written. The Light fails for a full minute; the Causeway Bell is rung. |
| 16 March, present — spring tide, dawn (Chapter 3) | Creed audits. The third entry is made and read aloud at the turn of the tide. The *Marigold* goes onto the Drowned Page and may never be entered again. |
