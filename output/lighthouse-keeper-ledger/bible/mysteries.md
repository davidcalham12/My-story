# Mysteries

- **Who are the ships the Commissioners cannot name?**
  - True: Ada Rowe invents no vessel. She copies the names off the Ridge petitions left on the seventh step — boats lost before the returns were kept, boats never licensed, the drowned street's own small craft — and enters them as sightings within the hour, in her own hand, at a bearing she chooses. It is a forgery of the record kept on behalf of people who may not climb the stair. She is not mad and has never broken a rule as written.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, Nell's petition on the step and Ada's line going into the Register an hour later. Lands: Chapter 3, when Creed asks her outright at the audit and she says so without defence.

- **Whose hand wrote the *Marigold* on page 91?**
  - True: Walter Byrd's. He wrote it at three of the clock on 3 March eleven years ago, was drowned on the causeway four hours later, and the *Marigold* did not sail until ten that night — nineteen hours after the line that records her passing. The entry is genuine, in the proper hand, inside the proper hour of a sighting that had not yet happened. No one witnessed the page, so it was never voided, and it cannot be struck out. This is true and the book does not explain it away.
  - Planted: Chapter 2, Ada finds the squared backward hand and *three of the clock* where she is certain she wrote nothing. Lands: Chapter 3, when Creed's own returns date the sailing and the two times are read aloud together at the turn of the tide.

- **What has Nell Harker been asking for, all these years, at every low water?**
  - True: the third entry. A name the Commissioners cannot match three times goes onto the Drowned Page, where it is closed forever — and where Creed must sign it. Nell is trading the name's future for its only official admission that the *Marigold* was ever afloat and took her husband down. Ada has been withholding the third line for two years to keep the name alive.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, Nell refusing to say the boat's name aloud and Ada refusing to write it. Lands: Chapter 3, Ada makes the entry, rings the Causeway Bell, and the name goes to the back of the Register.
