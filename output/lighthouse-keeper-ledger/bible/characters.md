# Characters — Sallowmere Reach

- **Ada Rowe** — keeper of the Corbie Light, eleven years on the post; exact, unrepentant
  - Wants: the drowned boats of Sallowmere to stand in writing, since nothing here stands otherwise.
  - Will do: copy names off the petitions on the seventh step and enter them as sightings, in her own hand, within the hour, so the forgery breaks no rule but the truth.
  - Tag: wipes the nib on the inside of her cuff before every line; her left cuff is black to the elbow.

- **Thomas Creed** — Commissioner of the Lower Light, auditor at every spring tide; patient, literal
  - Wants: the Register to agree with his returns, or the post withdrawn from a keeper who sights vessels nobody can name.
  - Will do: count oil against hours burned aloud, page by page, and sign the Drowned Page rather than leave a name open.
  - Tag: reads figures out at half voice as he writes them, and stops mid-sum when interrupted.

- **Nell Harker** — Ridge household; widow of the *Marigold*'s mate; stubborn, sparing of speech
  - Wants: the *Marigold* entered a third time, so the Commissioners must write her onto the Drowned Page and admit she was ever afloat.
  - Will do: leave petitions on the seventh step at every low water and never climb past it, and never say the boat's name aloud until the Register says it first.
  - Tag: leaves her petitions weighted with a scrap of net float; says "if it's written" instead of yes.

- **Walter Byrd** — keeper before Ada Rowe, drowned on the causeway eleven years ago; meticulous, absent
  - Wanted: the character of the Light kept, two flashes and a dark, whatever came up the stair.
  - Did: wrote the *Marigold* onto page 91 at three in the morning, nineteen hours before she sailed, and never struck a line out in thirty years.
  - Tag: a squared, backward-leaning hand with the hour written in full — *three of the clock* — where Ada writes 3.00.
