16 March, dawn, spring tide. Creed audited the Register, stopped mid-sum at page 91, and asked outright where the unmatched names came from; Ada told him she copies them off the petitions on the seventh step and offered no defence. His return dated the *Marigold*'s sailing at ten of the clock, nineteen hours after Walter Byrd's line. The two hours were read aloud at the turn of the tide and neither of them accounted for it. Ada entered the *Marigold* a third time from that morning's petition, wrote the name onto the Drowned Page herself, and Creed countersigned beneath her. She read the line down to Nell Harker at the seventh step, and Nell said the name once, aloud, over the water.
