15 March, night. Turning back to page 91 before Creed's audit, Ada found a new line beneath Walter Byrd's entry, in Walter's hand — *three of the clock, sou'-sou'-west, Marigold, ketch*. A line in a hand not the keeper's voids the page and counts toward no tally, so the *Marigold* still stands at two. The Light failed a full minute and Ada rang the Causeway Bell until the character came back. Nell Harker came to the seventh step and said the boat's name aloud for the first time in eleven years. Before high water Ada recopied page 91 entire, Walter's line included, and Nell and a second Ridge witness signed the fair copy on the step.
