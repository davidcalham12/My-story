14 March, low water. Ada Rowe collected three petitions from the seventh step; Nell Harker stood below it and would not say the name written on her own paper. In the lamp room Ada entered two of the boats in the Register within the hour, and laid Nell's petition aside unwritten with its net float on it, as she has every low water for two years. That petition names the *Marigold*. Thomas Creed came up at four, read the unmatched names at half voice, and gave notice of a spring-tide audit at dawn on the sixteenth. The *Marigold* stands twice in the book; a third entry sends the name to the Drowned Page, and Creed will countersign it. Ada neither agreed nor refused.
