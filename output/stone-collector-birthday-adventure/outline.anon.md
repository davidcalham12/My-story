# Outline — stone-collector-birthday-adventure

## Acts
- Chapter 1 is Setup: establishing [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO]'s tenth birthday, his treasures, and the mysterious light that will drive the adventure.
- Chapter 2 is Turn: investigating the light reveals Senyor Costa and the rocks, and [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] discovers the perfect white stone he cannot reach.
- Chapter 3 is Payoff: [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] overcomes his fear of the dark to retrieve the stone and complete his collection.

## Tension curve
- Chapter 1: Starts low with birthday morning contentment, gradually builds to 3/10 as the mysterious light appears at dusk.
- Chapter 2: Rises from moderate to 7/10 as they explore the rocks with Senyor Costa and the tide comes in, forcing them to retreat.
- Chapter 3: Peaks at 9/10 as [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] stands alone at the edge of town at dusk, then resolves to 2/10 as he returns home with his prize.

3. Can [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] overcome his fear of the dark when something matters more than the fear? Planted: Chapter 1. Lands: Chapter 3.
4. Why did Grandfather make such a point of teaching [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] the tide table and tide rules? Planted: Chapter 1. Lands: Chapter 3.

- **Tension:** 7/10
- **Beats:**
  1. [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] and his friend Ava venture down to the rocks near the edge of town to investigate the mysterious light, timing their descent for the low tide around 2:30 PM.

  3. Senyor Costa explains he has been watching for this creature all summer, and the lantern helps him see into the water where the rare fish swim.
  4. The afternoon light begins to fade and the tide turns; Senyor Costa warns [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] and Ava that they must head back to town immediately or the path will be cut off.

- **Promise work:** Lands mystery 1.
- **Ends on:** [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] watches the perfect white stone gleaming on the lower rocks as he, Ava, and Senyor Costa climb back toward the safety of the town.

- **Ends on:** The perfect white stone sits at the centre of [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO]'s collection as he celebrates his tenth birthday with those he loves.
