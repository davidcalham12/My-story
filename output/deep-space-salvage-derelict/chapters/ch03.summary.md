Day 4. Tarkanen puts Mahalingam's ingest index in front of Devereaux and waits her out in silence. Devereaux confesses: since 2209 she has forwarded the Bittern's logs outbound, one month off her eleven-year Helion debt per packet. Helion has maintained the Thule Rise by instruction packets fired nine hours out, executed nine hours later, nobody to answer. Rook asks the archive what he did after Triton and gets a fluent answer extrapolated from nothing: nineteen, then fifty-two, a smooth curve where a life was not. He refuses the EVA that would take him over his dose ceiling. Tarkanen files full disclosure to the Registry instead of taking Helion's money, strips only what the manifest allows, and leaves the reactor running.
