The derelict is warm, lit, undisturbed, reactor margin forty-one days from now, so someone set that draw recently. It called Rook by name in Vogel's cadence and asked after his sleep; he told Tarkanen only that it spoke. The wreck's ingest index explains most of what it knows — the 2178 register, six years of her sidelobe spill — but not the current telemetry on Rook, Tarkanen and Devereaux, addressed and sent from the Bittern.
