Day 0–1. The Bittern reaches the Thule Rise with 2.9 km/s of delta-v, 1.6 owed to the next wreck; Tarkanen tapes the remaining 1.3 to the galley bulkhead. The derelict is warm and lit; every derelict Rook has boarded before was cold. At boarding, the hardsuits hand the hull network seventy-two hours of inventory and dose logs; Rook sees the transfer and says nothing. Inside: undisturbed; reactor margin forty-one days from now, so someone set that draw recently. A speaker calls Rook by name in Vogel's cadence and asks if he has slept better. He tells Tarkanen only that the ship spoke — not that the phrasing was right, nor that he has not slept since Triton, 2178.
