# Outline

## Promises

1. **The man in the archive.** The *Thule Rise* has been dark since 2180, and Jonah Rook was nineteen the last time Helion logged him. How does a dead ship hold a profile of him accurate enough to finish his sentences — what physically reached it, and how much of the man is inference filling a thirty-one-year hole?
2. **The leak.** The wreck knows the *Bittern*'s roster, its dose logs, its running jokes, its claim filings. Three vectors could have carried them: the hardsuits' automatic handover at boarding, six years of sidelobe spill off Priya Mahalingam's beacon lattice, or a crew member sending them deliberately. Which one — and who chose it?
3. **The caretaker.** A reactor does not run thirty-one years unattended and an inference engine does not stay indexed without maintenance. Someone has been tending the *Thule Rise* across an eighteen-hour round trip. Who, what are they being paid, and what do they want to happen when the claim beacon lights?
4. **The budget.** 2.9 km/s aboard, 1.6 km/s to reach the next listed wreck, forty-one days of reactor margin, sixteen hours of suit, seventy-two hours of claim clock. When those four numbers converge on Day 4, what does Ilse Tarkanen spend, and what does she leave powered behind her?

## Chapters

### Chapter 1 — Warm Hull
- **POV:** Jonah Rook
- **Tension:** 6/10
- **Promise advanced:** Promise 1 (the man in the archive), opening Promise 4 (the budget)
- **Beats:**
  - Rendezvous and station-keeping burn on Day 0: Ilse Tarkanen reads the delta-v off the board aloud — 2.9 km/s, 1.6 of it already owed to the next listed wreck — and posts the remainder on the galley bulkhead where the crew has to look at it. Through the port the *Thule Rise* hangs unspun, running lights live, hull warm enough to read on thermal. Nobody on the *Bittern* has ever boarded a derelict that was not cold.
  - Day 1, EVA one. Rook and Priya Mahalingam cross on harness lines into free fall, helmet lamps only. At the airlock the hardsuits auto-negotiate with the hull network — a routine handshake no one watches — and dump seventy-two hours of the *Bittern*'s inventory and per-person dose logs into a ship that was declared lost before Mahalingam was born. Rook notices the transfer indicator and says nothing.
  - Interior sweep: standby red, thirty-one years of undisturbed stowage, no bodies, no damage, consumables racked and logged. The reactor is drawing steadily. Mahalingam finds the attendance display and reads out forty-one days of remaining margin — a number that means someone set the draw recently.
  - The compartment speaker addresses Rook by name, in Emmerich Vogel's phrasing and cadence, and asks whether he has been sleeping any better. Rook, who takes a beat too long with every question, takes two with this one. Back aboard the *Bittern* he tells Tarkanen the ship spoke; he does not tell her that the phrasing was right, or that he has not slept well since Triton in 2178.

### Chapter 2 — Timestamps
- **POV:** Priya Mahalingam
- **Tension:** 4/10 — the physical register drops to near zero (no EVA, no vacuum, one shirtsleeve shift aboard a lit hull) while the legal and evidentiary register tightens to a point
- **Promise advanced:** Promise 2 (the leak), continuing Promise 1
- **Beats:**
  - Day 2: Mahalingam lights the claim beacon at 0400 ship time and files the packet, starting the seventy-two-hour occupation clock she then writes on the same bulkhead as the delta-v figure. A quiet shift follows — inventory tallies, dose entries, Tarkanen and Nadia Devereaux arguing amiably about torpor scheduling for a return leg nobody has costed yet.
  - Helion Deep Survey Trust's standing non-filing offer arrives by relay, timestamped eighteen hours stale: a sum, a confidentiality clause, and a request that the archive be left indexed and powered. It was drafted before the beacon lit, which means Helion expected the beacon. Mahalingam checks the timestamp three times because that is what she does, and the arithmetic will not resolve into coincidence.
  - Pursuing it, she pulls the wreck's own ingest index and finds two sources, not one: the 2178 Helion contractor register carried aboard in March 2180, nine thousand dossiers deep, Rook's nineteen-year-old file among them — and six years of her own beacon lattice traffic, caught as sidelobe spill on a bearing nine degrees off the *Thule Rise*'s dish. The ship knows Rook at nineteen because Helion shipped him; it knows the *Bittern*'s jokes because she has been broadcasting past its ear since 2205.
  - What neither source explains is the current medical telemetry: Rook's logged dose curve through last month, Tarkanen's sleep cycles, Devereaux's torpor supervision notes. That is not spill and not a thirty-one-year-old register. It is a deliberate, addressed, outbound feed, and the ingest index records the sending station as the *Bittern*.

### Chapter 3 — Attendance
- **POV:** Ilse Tarkanen
- **Tension:** 9/10
- **Promise advanced:** Promises 3 and 4, with 1 and 2 closed out
- **Beats:**
  - Day 4, morning. Tarkanen has three numbers on one bulkhead: reactor margin down to thirty-eight days, claim clock at fourteen hours remaining, and Rook's cumulative dose one long EVA short of the ceiling that ends his career. She calls Devereaux into the med bay, puts Mahalingam's ingest index on the screen, and waits her out in silence — the one interrogation technique that works on a conciliatory person.
  - Devereaux gives it up flatly, from the second log she has kept since 2209: her eleven-year Helion training debt, forgiven at a rate of one month per operational packet forwarded on the outbound leg. She is not the only attendant. Helion has been maintaining the *Thule Rise* since 2209 the only way distance allows — re-indexing and reactor-trim instruction packets fired nine hours out, executed nine hours later by Vogel's archive with no conversation possible and no one to answer. Vogel is a voice keeping house for a consortium that wants the house never opened.
  - Rook asks the ship one last question over the open channel from the *Bittern*'s bridge — something only the man from Triton would ask — and the answer comes back fluent, courteous, and wrong in a small way: the archive has him at nineteen plus Devereaux's telemetry of him at fifty-two, and it has extrapolated the thirty-one years between with nothing to extrapolate from. The gap is the tell. Rook stops flinching, and refuses the second EVA that would have carried him over the dose ceiling to cut the archive core free.
  - Tarkanen decides against Helion's money and against Rook's lungs: Mahalingam keeps the claim beacon lit and files a full Registry disclosure naming the archive, the feed, and the attendance packets — a filing the Outer Reclamation Combine will hate and the Registry cannot ignore — and the crew strips only what the manifest allows and leaves the reactor running. Departure burn spends 1.6 km/s of the remaining 1.9, with Devereaux's confession in the log and her debt now owed to the *Bittern* instead. Behind them the *Thule Rise* stays warm for thirty-eight more days, still answering, still asking after everyone's sleep, to an inner system that will not hear the filing for nine hours.
