# Timeline

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 2151 | The outer-system survey boom collapses. Helion Deep Survey Trust stops launching and starts litigating; the wreck field begins accumulating. |
| 2174 | Helion writes the survey-era data-retention standard still in use: every survey vessel departs carrying the Trust's full contractor register — voiceprints, medical telemetry, psychometric dossiers — as a crew-replacement contingency. |
| 2178 | Jonah Rook, aged nineteen, signs into the Helion contractor pool on a Triton resupply run. His dossier enters the register that year. He never flies for Helion again. |
| 2180, March | The Thule Rise departs under Emmerich Vogel with the 2178 register aboard, Rook's file among nine thousand others. |
| 2180, December | Last received transmission from the Thule Rise. Helion declares the ship lost; the Registry of Wreck and Abandonment lists it unsalvageable and unlocated. |
| 2200 | Nadia Devereaux completes Helion-funded medical training and accrues an eleven-year service debt she has not discharged. |
| 2205 | The Bittern's current crew forms under Ilse Tarkanen. Priya Mahalingam begins tight-beaming Combine beacon traffic and Registry claim packets on the lattice bearing — a line that passes within nine degrees of the Thule Rise's dish. |
| 2209 | Helion resumes tight-beam contact with the Thule Rise: re-indexing packets, nine hours out, nine hours back, no conversation possible. Devereaux begins forwarding the Bittern's operational logs on the outbound leg. |
| 2211, Day 0 | The Bittern makes rendezvous. Delta-v remaining: 2.9 km/s. Departure to the next listed wreck costs 1.6 km/s. The Thule Rise is powered, warm, unspun, and holds a reactor margin of forty-one days at current draw. |
| 2211, Day 1 | Boarding. Hardsuits auto-negotiate with the hull network and hand over seventy-two hours of inventory and dose logs. The wreck addresses Rook by name, in Vogel's phrasing, and asks after his sleep. |
| 2211, Day 2 | Mahalingam lights the claim beacon; the seventy-two-hour occupation clock starts. Helion's standing non-filing offer arrives by relay, timestamped eighteen hours stale. |
| 2211, Day 4 | Reactor margin, claim clock and suit dose converge; the crew decides what leaves the wreck with them and what stays powered behind them. |
