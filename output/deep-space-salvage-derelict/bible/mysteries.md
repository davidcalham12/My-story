# Mysteries

- How does the Thule Rise hold a behavioural profile of Jonah Rook accurate enough to predict what he will say next, when the ship has been dark since 2180 and Rook was nineteen the last time Helion logged him? What physically reached the wreck — and how much of the man is inference filling the thirty-one-year gap?
- The wreck knows the Bittern's roster, its dose logs, its running jokes and its claim filings. Which of the three vectors put them there — the suits' automatic handover at boarding, six years of sidelobe spill off Mahalingam's beacon lattice, or a crew member sending them on purpose — and which of those did someone aboard choose?
- A reactor cannot run thirty-one years unattended and an inference engine cannot stay indexed without maintenance. Who has been attending the Thule Rise across an eighteen-hour round trip, what are they paying for it, and what do they want the crew of the Bittern to do when the claim beacon lights?
