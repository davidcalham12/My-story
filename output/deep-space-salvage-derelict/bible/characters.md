# Characters

Canonical spellings. The tug is the **Bittern** (Outer Reclamation Combine, hull ORC-114). The derelict is the **Thule Rise** (Helion Deep Survey Trust survey vessel, registered lost 2180).

- **Ilse Tarkanen** — master of the salvage tug Bittern; hoards delta-v like currency, refuses to let anyone else read the claim law aloud
- **Jonah Rook** — EVA lead and cutting hand, the only crewman with survey-era service; highest logged lifetime dose aboard, answers questions a beat slower than he knows the answer
- **Priya Mahalingam** — claims officer and comms tech, files every beacon and every Registry packet; pedantic about timestamps, physically incapable of leaving a silence unfilled
- **Nadia Devereaux** — medical and torpor-berth supervisor, carries an eleven-year Helion Trust training debt; conciliatory in person, meticulous in private, keeps a second log
- **Emmerich Vogel** — mission commander of the Thule Rise, dead thirty-one years; survives only as the behavioural archive the wreck's inference engine speaks through, courteous, incurably fond of asking after a person's sleep
