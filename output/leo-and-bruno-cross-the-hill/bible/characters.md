- **Leo** — protagonist; curious about what lies beyond his known world, secretly afraid of the dark though he will not admit it

- **Bruno** — terrier companion; loyal to Leo without question, but will refuse to cross into situations that genuinely terrify him

- **Grandfather** — mentor figure; patient observer of stars and geology, encourages curiosity tempered with real caution

- **Elena** — Leo's mother; protective of his safety, but respects his need to grow and learn through honest risk

- **Mateo** — Leo's best friend from the lane; bold with dares, but carries the weight of his own dare when Leo actually crosses

- **Rosa** — a girl from the far side; matter-of-fact about her hills, unhurried in her ways
