- **What is on the other side of the hill?**
  - True: It is genuine geography—different stone, different plants, different light—but not magical or dangerous, just actual world that is distant from Leo's usual territory.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, when Mateo's dare and Leo's question become urgent and real.
  - Lands: Chapter 7, when Leo reaches the far side and explores it, learning it is simply different.

- **What was Captain Crunch when it was alive?**
  - True: A small marine animal from ancient seas that once covered this area; the fossil proves the ground Leo knows was once underwater.
  - Planted: Chapter 2, when Leo packs Captain Crunch and wonders about what it was.
  - Lands: Chapter 6, when Leo sees fossilized rock layers on the far side and understands Captain Crunch's place in that deep history.

- **Is the dark as dangerous as Leo secretly fears?**
  - True: Darkness is genuinely limiting—vision is restricted, getting lost is possible—but it is not evil or magical, and it is manageable with light, knowledge, and courage.
  - Planted: Chapter 2, when Elena sets the condition that Leo must be home before dark, revealing his secret fear.
  - Lands: Chapter 9, when Leo descends in darkness and proves the fear is real but manageable.

- **What was Grandfather thinking when he marked the sunset time?**
  - True: That Leo must know the real limits—exactly when darkness comes, how light changes—because curiosity backed by real knowledge is how a boy learns to trust himself. Grandfather believed crossing a hill teaches what staying home cannot, and that Leo must face fear with facts, not forbidding.
  - Planted: Chapter 5, when Grandfather marks the sunset time precisely and tells Leo to pay attention to the light.
  - Lands: Chapter 10, when Grandfather asks what Leo found, revealing he knew Leo would return and learn.

- **Will Bruno come all the way across the hill with Leo?**
  - True: Bruno goes as far as loyalty and courage allow, but his tiredness and fear give Leo a true measure of real limits and his own courage.
  - Planted: Chapter 3, when Leo and Bruno prepare for the journey, showing both Bruno's loyalty and his honest hesitation.
  - Lands: Chapter 8, when Bruno's exhaustion forces Leo to choose how far to push and teaches him about real, uncompromising limits.
