| When | What |
|---|---|
| Summer before this year | Grandfather visits and helps Leo build the cardboard observatory in the garden. |
| Spring of this year | Leo finds a fossil at the beach in Cadaques and names it Captain Crunch. |
| Early June (Friday) | Leo turns ten and asks Elena what is on the other side of the hill; that evening, Mateo dares him with a double-dog dare. |
| Early June (Saturday morning) | Leo asks Elena for permission to cross. She agrees with conditions: take Bruno, stay together, be home before dark, tell Grandfather. |
| Early June (Saturday afternoon) | Leo gathers supplies: Captain Crunch, smooth stones from his collection, packed lunch, torch with tired batteries. He prepares the bicycle. |
| Early June (Saturday evening) | Grandfather comes by and marks the exact time of sunset on his watch: 9:15 PM. He tells Leo to pay attention to the light. |
| Early June (Sunday, dawn) | Leo and Bruno set out on the bicycle toward the base of the hill. The morning is warm and bright. |
| Early June (Sunday, mid-morning) | Leo and Bruno reach the foot of the hill and begin to climb. The slope is steeper than it looked from far away. Bruno is tiring but still climbing. |
| Early June (Sunday, late morning) | Leo and Bruno reach the summit. Behind them: the village and the familiar beach. Ahead: the far side—genuinely different—different stone, different plants, different light. |
| Early June (Sunday, afternoon) | Leo and Bruno explore the far side. No danger, no magic, just actual geology and distance. The afternoon passes faster than Leo planned. |
| Early June (Sunday, late afternoon) | Leo checks the time Grandfather marked. The sun is lower than expected. Bruno is very tired and reluctant to continue. They are farther from home than Leo realized. |
| Early June (Sunday, late afternoon) | Leo and Bruno turn back toward the summit. They must climb again and descend to home before full darkness falls. The light is changing. Urgency is real. |
| Early June (Sunday, dusk) | From the summit on the return, Leo can see village lights coming on. The sky is deep blue. Darkness is closing in. The torch batteries are nearly dead. |
| Early June (Sunday, night and late evening) | Leo and Bruno descend the near side in darkness. The path is familiar from above but fear is real. They reach the village and then home. Elena is worried but not angry. Grandfather is waiting and asks what Leo found. Leo answers plainly and without drama, reporting what he saw rather than performing the adventure. |
