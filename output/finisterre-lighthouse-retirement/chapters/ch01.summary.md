The Sunday his watch formally ends. Before dawn [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] climbs the tower out of habit and winds the optic by hand though the motor no longer needs him. In the keeper's office he turns back to his own October 1987 entry — the supply failed, he hand-wound the light all night, five vessels sheltered in the beam — reads it, says nothing, writes the retirement entry and closes the book. In the greenhouse he saves seed from the last ripe tomatoes and labels the jars. At the Sunday meal he tells the octopus joke in its unchanged words; Rosa and Manuel know it before it arrives. At dusk he and Manuel climb to the lamp room and the key changes hands in silence.

Still open: what he has never said aloud about that night, and whether Manuel will ask.
