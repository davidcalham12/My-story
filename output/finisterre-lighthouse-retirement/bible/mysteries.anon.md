# Mysteries

- **Q1. Why does [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] still climb the lighthouse steps the morning after his retirement?**
- Planted: Chapter 1
- Lands: Chapter 1
- Answer: Habit and belonging—he climbs because he has always climbed, because the light is still burning, because his body knows no other way to begin a day.

- **Q2. Why is the Sunday joke about the octopus and the accountant told in exactly the same words, decade after decade?**
- Planted: Chapter 1
- Lands: Chapter 1
- Answer: It is perfect as it is; there is no better way to say it. And it is the one thing in his week that does not change.

- **Q3. What does [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] mean to do with the lighthouse key?**
- Planted: Chapter 1
- Lands: Chapter 1
- Answer: He passes it to Manuel at the hour the watch formally ends—not as a possession but as a witnessing, the burden of remembering that someone kept the light. The timing is what makes it lawful rather than theft.

- **Q4. Why does [NOMBRE_ANONIMIZADO] save seeds from the tomatoes in labeled jars, year after year?**
- Planted: Chapter 1
- Lands: Chapter 1
- Answer: To prove that he can make things grow from what is already his; the garden is a second light he tends.
