# Timeline: Leo and the Other Side of the Hill

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Two summers prior | Leo and his grandfather Samuel build a cardboard observatory in the garden |
| One summer prior (Cadaques beach) | Leo finds a fossil among the rocks and names it Captain Crunch |
| Throughout Leo's first decade | Leo collects smooth stones, learning to sort them by weight and color |
| Late spring | Leo asks directly and repeatedly: what is on the other side of the hill? |
| June 23rd | Leo turns ten; the summer of discovery begins |
| Early July | Leo cycles to the village edge most afternoons, studying the hill's slope |
| Mid-July | Iris challenges Leo to prove he is brave enough to reach the far side |
| Mid-July | Alice allows Leo to borrow the old bicycle; it is fast enough to matter |
| Late July | Leo finds his torch in a drawer and tests it; the battery is weak |
| Late July | Leo and Bruno reach the farmer's gate; Mr. Pike turns them back, angry |
| Late July | Leo finds a way around the boundary; the farmer does not follow him |
| Late July | Leo reaches the hilltop and discovers the other side: a stream, a meadow, and a shepherd's shelter |
| Early August | Leo returns home as darkness falls; his torch is almost gone |
| Early August | Leo tells Samuel what he found; he tells Iris and she listens with wonder |
