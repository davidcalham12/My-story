# Mysteries: Leo and the Other Side of the Hill

- **What is on the other side of the hill?**
  - True: A small stream winds through a wild meadow. An old stone shelter built long ago by shepherds stands near the water. The world beyond the village is larger, quieter, and more real than Leo ever imagined.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, when Leo asks the question aloud and the adults dismiss it.
  - Lands: Chapter 9, when Leo crosses the boundary and discovers the meadow, stream, and shelter.

- **Will Leo's fading torch last long enough?**
  - True: The torch works when Leo needs it, but the beam is very dim. It guides him through the darkness before his discovery but will fail soon after.
  - Planted: Chapter 2, when Leo finds the torch and tests its battery, discovering it is weak.
  - Lands: Chapter 9, when Leo uses the torch in the final darkness and feels it weakening.

- **What does the farmer truly intend toward trespassers?**
  - True: Mr. Pike guards his boundaries fiercely, but he is not cruel. He wants trespassers off his land, but he will not harm a child. Once Leo passes the gate, the farmer lets him go.
  - Planted: Chapter 3, when Mr. Pike blocks Leo at the gate and turns him back angrily.
  - Lands: Chapter 5, when Leo finds a way around the boundary and discovers the farmer does not pursue.

- **Can Leo overcome his fear of the dark?**
  - True: Leo discovers that by pausing to light the torch and name aloud one true thing he has noticed in the world, the darkness becomes bearable. His fear does not vanish, but it becomes something he can walk through.
  - Planted: Chapter 1, when Leo's secret fear of the dark is revealed.
  - Lands: Chapter 8, when Leo enters darkness and finds the strength to move forward.

- **Will Iris become Leo's ally or his betrayer?**
  - True: Iris learns that Leo went to the other side, but she does not tell the adults. She asks Leo to tell her what he found. She becomes his companion in wonder, not his rival.
  - Planted: Chapter 3, when Iris challenges Leo and they argue about who is brave enough.
  - Lands: Chapter 10, when Leo tells Iris his story and she listens with the same hunger for discovery he felt.
