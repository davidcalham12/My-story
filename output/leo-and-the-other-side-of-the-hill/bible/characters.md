# Characters: Leo and the Other Side of the Hill

- **Leo** — protagonist; curious, methodical in noticing small things, secretly afraid of the dark though he won't admit it

- **Bruno** — Leo's terrier; loyal, steady, understands what gates and boundaries mean, will follow Leo anywhere

- **Samuel** — Leo's grandfather; patient, builder of impossible things in gardens, knows the village history and its unasked questions

- **Alice** — Leo's mother; protective, keeps him close, expects him to obey the village's unwritten rules

- **Mr. Pike** — the farmer; respects his property above all else, immovable on trespassing, will turn Leo back without cruelty

- **Iris** — village child; challenges Leo, skeptical, becomes his ally and witness
