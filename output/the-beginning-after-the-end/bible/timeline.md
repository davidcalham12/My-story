# Timeline

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Nineteen summers ago | The quiet. Nobody in Harrow Bench explains it, then or since; forty-one people are what the shelf above the river is left holding. |
| Eighteen springs ago | Tom Leary is born, the first child after. He grows up answering questions the older people are never asked, with what he was told. |
| Eleven winters ago | The ledger is begun on the vestry table after a bad season — names, rows, debts of work. The two-signature rule is written the same week, and Martha Gale becomes one of the two. |
| Last autumn | The last walker before Ruth is fed, entered, given a bed and a row; he winters well and leaves in spring with a jar of Harrow seed that does not come back. Martha has not signed for a stranger since. |
| Thursday to Saturday, the three nights | Ruth Vance walks in over the bridge and is fed at the first house she reaches, the Learys'. Owen Pike makes up an inn bed and talks her case to anyone with their hands busy. On the second night she sets a short row of her own seed on the flats, unentered; Tom finds it at dawn while clearing the well pipe, and does not pull it up. |
| Sunday, the fourth morning | The meeting. Whether Ruth stays, whether the store is opened and by whose two signatures, and whose name goes in beside the row already growing on the flats. |
