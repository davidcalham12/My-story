# Characters

- **Martha Gale** — seed store keeper for the Orchard Side, one of the two names that must sign before the vestry shelves are opened; slow to agree, honest about her reasons
- **Owen Pike** — Road Side; airs the beds in the old inn and keeps the bridge road clear, and wants Ruth's name in the ledger before Sunday; talkative, stubborn in a friendly way
- **Ruth Vance** — the walker who came in over the bridge with a jar of her own seed and three nights to make a case; careful with words, plants before she asks
- **Tom Leary** — born the spring after the quiet, minds the upper well and its pipe, and is the one person in the valley people are allowed to ask; literal, watchful

## Wants, in one line each

- **Martha Gale** wants the flats left for walnut and winter wheat, and no stranger's row on them — and her signature is the only thing that opens the store.
- **Owen Pike** wants Ruth entered in the ledger, because a valley nobody arrives in has agreed to end.
- **Ruth Vance** wants one row on the flats under her own name, and will trade what is in her jar for it.
- **Tom Leary** wants his own line in the ledger instead of a row kept under his mother's name, and finds out on the second night what a name costs.
