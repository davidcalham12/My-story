# Mysteries

- What is in Ruth Vance's jar — what seed she carried over a road nobody mends, and whether it is something Harrow Bench once had and lost, or something the valley has never grown.
- Tom Leary found an unentered row on the flats and the rule says he should have pulled it up. Why did he leave it standing, and what does he want in exchange for having left it?
- Whose two signatures open the seed store on Sunday morning — and if Martha Gale is not one of them, what does she do with the walnut ground she has been saving since last autumn?
