Thursday, dusk. Ruth Vance walks into Harrow Bench with a stoppered jar of seed and is fed at the first house, the Learys'. She does not open the jar; nobody asks her name. Owen Pike airs her an inn bed: three nights, then Sunday's meeting decides. Ruth asks whether the flats above the upper well are spoken for; Tom Leary, born the spring after the quiet, answers, because he is the one allowed to be asked. Martha Gale comes to the door but not through it — the store opens after the meeting, two names, both signing — and has not signed for a stranger since last autumn. Alone in the yard, Ruth looks at the flats and decides something unannounced.
