Sunday, the fourth morning. The meeting runs through a slate and the bridge road before it reaches Ruth. Owen Pike makes her case; Martha Gale says a tree planted for a stranger is a tree the valley does not eat from, and that she is not signing. Tom Leary stands and tells the room he found the unentered row and did not pull it up, and asks for his own line in the ledger instead of his mother's. Ruth names the seed — skirret, which the valley grew once and stopped — and says the jar goes to the store either way. Martha signs, and names Tom as the second signature. Ruth's name goes in beside the row; the walnut ground stays walnut.
