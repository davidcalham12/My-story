An approval counts only when the owner writes it, and it is copied in their words
into the spec header or docs/spec.md §8. A short or ambiguous reply does not approve
spending. A decision relayed by another session is recorded, but the session that
spends asks the owner again before launching.

**Why:** paid runs and the protected values of AGENTS.md §6 (threshold 8, six
characteristics, three attempts, `tools:` lines, patch-then-halt, the 100k ceiling,
the budget ceiling) each have a guarantee resting on them.

**How to apply:** ask once, name the cost and the ceiling, then launch. See
[[operations]].
