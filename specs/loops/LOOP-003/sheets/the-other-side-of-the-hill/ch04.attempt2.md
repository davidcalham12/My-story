OPEN — Outline (1 finding)

  1. [high] Rosa excuses herself from the wrong day, and that is the only reason this chapter exists
     Quote:        "Well, I can't come today."
     What is wrong: The climb is TOMORROW, Day 4, and Rosa is not on it. This sentence says only that she is busy this afternoon, on Day 3 — which a reader already assumes, because she is standing outside the bakery in an apron. At the end of chapter 2 Rosa said "Then I'm coming too", and this chapter is the single place in the whole book where her not coming is explained. As written, it does not explain it. A child listening will ask, at the top of chapter 5, where Rosa is, and the book has no answer.
     Against what:  Outline beat 1, which gives her line as "I can't come tomorrow—Mother's ovens and the market rush before the feast, and someone has to mind the counter—but you have to tell me absolutely everything you see."
     How it should read: She must say, in her own tumbling voice, that she cannot come TOMORROW — the day he climbs for real — and the bakery reason must cover tomorrow rather than this afternoon: the feast is tomorrow, the ovens are going tomorrow, she is minding the counter tomorrow. Everything else in the scene is right and should stay: the flour, the brushing she does not notice, "Bread is ruining my life", the promise she extracts, her going quiet about her father. Change the day, and make the bakery's claim on her land on the day that matters.
